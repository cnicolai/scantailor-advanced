Scenarios (fixed here):
- Input: `PageSpec{MIXED, dpi, seed 1, skewDeg 0}` for dpi 300 and 600; `outputDpi` 600; layout `SINGLE_PAGE_UNCUT` (research P3: AUTO mis-cuts this page and drops the picture).
- Mapping truth to output: `renderInkMask(spec, 600, 0)` (text ink of the whole flat page at 600 dpi), `registerMask(output, mask, 4)` gives the offset; a flat-frame rectangle maps to output as `rect` scaled by 600 / input DPI, translated by the offset.
- `_bw`: `BLACK_AND_WHITE`, despeckle 1.0 (app default) and a second run with despeckle 0.0 (off) for the truth check only. A speck is present if its mapped rectangle, grown by 1 px, holds at least one ink pixel. Truth: share of specks present with despeckle off is reported (fixture sanity: at least 90%); removed share = 1 − present(default) / present(off) ≥ 0.90; glyph components: number of 8-connected ink components inside the mapped text block differs by at most 1% between the two runs.
- `_color`: `COLOR_GRAYSCALE`. Truth: mean chroma (max − min channel) inside the mapped picture rectangle shrunk by 4 px ≥ 0.5 x the input picture's mean chroma; mean grey under registered text ink ≤ mean grey under text-block paper − 100.
- `_mixed` (300 dpi only, D-04): `MIXED`. Truth: share of non-bi-level pixels (not all channels ≤ 5 and not all channels ≥ 250) inside the mapped picture rectangle shrunk by 4 px ≥ 0.95; share of pure black or pure white pixels (all channels 0 or all 255) outside the mapped picture rectangle grown by 8 px ≥ 0.999.
- AUTO observation: `test_mixed_auto_layout_keeps_single_page` runs the 300 dpi page with AUTO layout in `BLACK_AND_WHITE`; truth: `layoutType == 0` (SINGLE_PAGE_UNCUT) and ink inside the mapped picture rectangle. Research measured SINGLE_PAGE_CUT with the picture dropped, so expect `XFAIL_CHECK(..., "page_split AUTO cuts the mixed page and drops its picture: no requirement")` and a finding. No golden for this case.
- Starting bounds above (90%, 1%, 0.5x chroma, 100 levels, 95%, 99.9%) are this plan's starting tolerances; the D-15 tightening rule and D-18 apply to them as to the D-15 values.
- Goldens: `mixed_300_bw`, `mixed_600_bw` (BILEVEL), `mixed_300_color`, `mixed_600_color`, `mixed_300_mixed` (COLOR), each with a text golden (keys of plan 02-05's text goldens plus `color_mode` and `despeckle_level`), and `fixtures` with keys `mixed_300`, `mixed_600`.
- Sanity per golden (D-05): portrait; ink fraction 0.5%..60% (the binarized picture adds ink); width between 2 x text-block width and 2 x page width x 1.2; content where the truth puts it (registration mismatch ≤ 5% of truth text ink at the found offset); for `_color` and `_mixed` the output is not bi-level.
