Conventions for the review-fix plans 02-11 to 02-14 (they extend "Conventions for every plan of this phase" in 02-01-PLAN.md):

- Long commands (a Debug or ASan build, a full Debug ctest) may exceed the tool time limit: run them in the background and wait for completion.
