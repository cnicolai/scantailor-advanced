Scenarios (fixed here):
- Inputs: `PageSpec{FLAT, dpi, seed 1, skewDeg 1.5}` for dpi 300 and 600; the deskew sweep uses seed 1 and the angles listed in must_haves.
- Pipeline: defaults plus `outputDpi` 600 (plan 02-01 decision), AUTO layout, despeckle 1.0 (app default); `colorMode` `BLACK_AND_WHITE` for `_bw`, `COLOR_GRAYSCALE` for `_color`. The sweep uses `LastStage::DESKEW`.
- Golden names: `flat_300_bw`, `flat_300_color`, `flat_600_bw`, `flat_600_color` (kind BILEVEL for `_bw`, COLOR for `_color`), text goldens with the same names, and `fixtures` (keys `flat_<dpi>_skew_<angle>` with the angle written as for example `p1p5` or `m3p0`, values from `fingerprint()`).
- Text-golden keys per scenario (suffix decides the tolerance, D-14): `seed`, `input_dpi`, `applied_skew_deg`, `deskew_angle_deg`, `layout_type`, `content_x_px`, `content_y_px`, `content_w_px`, `content_h_px`, `page_rect_x_px`, `page_rect_y_px`, `page_rect_w_px`, `page_rect_h_px`, `hard_margin_left_mm`, `hard_margin_top_mm`, `hard_margin_right_mm`, `hard_margin_bottom_mm`, `output_w_px`, `output_h_px`, `output_dpi`. Numbers through `formatNumber`.
- Truth checks: deskew |stored − expected| ≤ 0.15° (sign convention documented in `PageGenerator.h` by plan 02-01); content IoU via `centredBoxIoU` ≥ 0.95; for `_bw`, binarization: render `renderInkMask(spec, 600, netRotation)` where `netRotation` is the residual rotation left after deskew (applied angle plus the stored correction, in the generator's convention), `registerMask(output, mask, 4)`, then `truthMismatchFraction(output, mask, offset, edgeRadius)` with `edgeRadius` = output DPI / input DPI (2 for 300 dpi input, 1 for 600 dpi input), bound ≤ 0.005; for `_color`, contrast: mean grey of output pixels under registered truth ink ≤ mean grey under truth paper − 100.
- Sanity before any golden is written (D-05), via `outputSanity` plus two checks in the test: portrait; ink fraction 0.5%..50%; width between 2 x truth text-block width and 2 x page width x 1.2; `countHorizontalBands` over `nonBackgroundBox(output)` equals `lineCount` ± 1; the output's `nonBackgroundBox` left and top lie within 5 mm (at 600 dpi) of the stored left and top hard margins. The sanity result is passed to `CHECK_IMAGE_GOLDEN` and `CHECK_TEXT_GOLDEN` as `Sanity{ok, reason}`.
