**D-15 tightening rule (shared by plans 02-04 to 02-07).** For each ground-truth check, run it on all its fixtures in Release and record the worst-case error in the plan's records file. If the worst case is at most half the starting tolerance, set the tolerance to twice the worst case, rounded up to the check's step (angle 0.01°, pixels 0.5 px, percentages 0.05 percentage points, IoU 0.005 on 1 − IoU), never below one step. Otherwise keep the starting tolerance. If the worst case exceeds the starting tolerance, D-18 applies: the assertion becomes `XFAIL_CHECK(<condition>, "<defect>: <fixing requirement or 'no requirement'>")`, the measurement goes into the records file as a finding, and any golden records current behaviour. A tolerance is never loosened past its starting value and a fixture is never changed to avoid a mark (user decisions D-15 and D-18; research suggested re-basing unreachable tolerances, which these decisions do not allow).

**Findings format (shared by plans 02-04 to 02-08; plan 02-10 consolidates them and creates the backlog items).** Under `## Findings` in the plan's records file, one block per defect:
`### F-<plan>-<n>: <title>`, then lines `- Check:` (suite/case and assertion), `- Measured:` and `- Bound:`, `- Fixture:` (kind, DPI, seed), `- Mark:` (the exact XFAIL_CHECK tag), `- Fixing requirement:` (an ID such as SAFE-02, or `none`), `- Backlog: pending (plan 02-10)`. With no defect, write `None.` under the heading.
