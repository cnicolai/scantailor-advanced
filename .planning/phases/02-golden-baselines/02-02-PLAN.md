Design rules fixed at planning time:
- The `.cpp` files of `test_support` never include Boost.Test; they return results. Boost-dependent helpers are header-only (`GoldenChecks.h`, `ExpectedFailure.h`) and are included only by test sources.
- Golden layout: `<GOLDEN_DIR>/<suite>/<name>.png` and `<name>.txt`, with one `MANIFEST.txt` per suite directory, so plans 02-05, 02-06 and 02-07 can record their suites in parallel without touching a shared file. Suite and name must match `^[a-z0-9_]+$`.
- Failure artifacts: `<ARTIFACT_DIR>/<suite>/<name>/expected.png|actual.png|diff.png` (text: `expected.txt|actual.txt|diff.txt`), D-08.
- Log line format, parsed by plan 02-09 to measure the Release/Debug spread, printed for every metric of every comparison whether it passes or fails: `GOLDEN-METRIC suite=<suite> name=<name> kind=<bilevel|color|text> metric=<metric> value=<value> limit=<limit> result=<pass|fail>`, numbers with `QString::number(v, 'g', 10)`.
- Tolerance ceilings (D-12, D-14, research ARCHITECTURE.md Pattern 3): bi-level edge-tolerant XOR ≤ 0.1% of pixels; foreground connected-component count within ±2% (relative to the expected image's count); grey/colour MAE ≤ 0.5 and max error ≤ 8 levels; the shift check always requires (0, 0); text goldens 0.01° for `_deg`, 1 px for `_px`, 0.05 mm for `_mm`, exact otherwise. These are the starting values; plan 02-09 measures the Release/Debug spread and freezes the image-kind limits just above it, capped by these ceilings.
- Shift check (needed because an edge-tolerant XOR alone cannot see a 1-px shift, and D-13 requires a shifted golden to fail): over the image shrunk by 2 px on each side, compute the raw mismatch for every offset (dx, dy) in [-2, 2]² (bi-level: differing pixel count; colour: sum of absolute channel differences); the metric `shift` is 1 when some non-zero offset gives a strictly smaller mismatch than (0, 0), else 0; limit 0.
