Why the review's "dpi % 75 == 0" hint is rejected (user decision in the UAT): `scaled()` is `(base * dpi + 150) / 300` with base unit 1/300 in and the glyph dot `kDot = 5`, so the dot is exact only where 5 * dpi is a multiple of 300 (every multiple of 60). At 150 dpi the dot is 3 px instead of 2.5 and at 225 dpi 4 px instead of 3.75; the dot is then multiplied by up to 125 cells and 117 line dots while the page size, block origins and frames are rounded once, so the block outgrows its frame or the page.

The fit rule (Claude's discretion, chosen at planning time): a dpi is supported for a kind when 75 <= dpi <= 1200 and, with the geometry computed exactly as `computeGeometry` does, (a) every text block's nominal ink box, QRect(originX, originY, inkWidth(dot), inkHeight(dot)), lies inside QRect(0, 0, width, height); (b) that ink box lies inside the block's documented frame: SPREAD QRect(originX, originY, scaled(640), scaled(860)) per block, MIXED QRect(originX, originY, scaled(420), scaled(850)) (the frame `placeSpecks` already uses for speck clearance), CURVED the 700 x 800 frame centred on the 1000 x 1150 page, QRect(scaled(150), scaled(175), scaled(700), scaled(800)); FLAT documents no frame and gets (a) only; (c) the MIXED picture rectangle lies inside the page. Part (a) is the memory-safety condition. Parts (b) and (c) keep the ground truth honest: the SPREAD gutter band starts where the left frame ends, and speck clearance from text is measured from the MIXED frame, so a block that outgrows its frame makes the truth wrong without any out-of-bounds write (at 225 dpi the SPREAD left block runs about 20 px into the gutter band, the MIXED text about 17 px past its frame).
