## Facts at planning time (HEAD f6cc4457) and from earlier plans

- `mac_check` (plans 05-02 and 05-05) has `Summary`, `waitFor`, `--section` lists with `menubar`, `layout`, `keys` and `zone`, the activation sequence of `runMenubarSection`, the `cocoaprobe` API (`keyCodeFor`, `sendKey`, `sendMouse`, `mainMenu`, `activate`, `runLater`) and, in its anonymous namespace, copies of the test helpers `makeZoneEditor`, `addRectZone`, `zoneCount` and `makeContentView` (plan 05-05).
- `ZoneContextMenuInteraction` (src/core/zones/ZoneContextMenuInteraction.cpp) builds a `QMenu` child of the image view (`m_menu`, line 72) with the actions `Properties` and `Delete` per zone, pops it up at `lastContextMenuScreenPos()` or `QCursor::pos()` (lines 118-121), connects `aboutToHide` queued to `menuAboutToHide()` (line 115), and `menuAboutToHide()` (lines 135-155) waits once for 200 ms (`m_extraDelaysDone`, `QTimer::singleShot(200, ...)`) before restoring the default interaction when no item was triggered. The delay has no `Q_OS_MAC` guard (RESEARCH; CONTEXT's wording is outdated). The `Delete` item removes the zone at once (`deleteRequest`, lines 180-184).
- Qt turns a Control + left mouse down into a right button on Cocoa (`qnsview_mouse.mm`), and a two-finger click arrives as `rightMouseDown`; both reach `ImageViewBase::contextMenuEvent` (RESEARCH "Don't Hand-Roll", Pitfall 8).
- `TestMacQt::testRightClickOpensZoneMenu` (plan 05-04) opens the zone menu offscreen with a right-button press; the conditional test of Task 2 starts from it.

## Reconciliations

- CONTEXT calls the 200 ms delay a `Q_OS_MAC` workaround; RESEARCH found it unconditional. The decision rule is UI-SPEC's (keep unless always unnecessary), and the measurement compares `triggered()` with the queued `aboutToHide` slot, the order the existing code relies on.
- Two-finger click: the first VALIDATION draft listed it as manual-only; D-11 rules out manual steps, and the user is not asked to click. The NSEvent of a two-finger click and of a right-click is the same `rightMouseDown`, so the right-click row stands for both. That makes the two-finger row the one MAC-08 row without its own injected event: `records/context.md` names it as an unverified residual, `05-VALIDATION.md` lists it under "Unverified residuals", and the closing plan 05-11 repeats it in "For the user".
