- **A1**: Qt's Cocoa plugin delivers `QEvent::DevicePixelRatioChange` when a window is dragged between displays of different backing scale. Only one display is attached, so this cannot be observed (D-03); the offscreen two-screen tests prove the handlers, and every rebuilt buffer also compares its stored ratio at paint time, which covers a missed event. The closing record says so.
- **A7**: `QT_SCREEN_SCALE_FACTORS` stays honoured by the offscreen platform in later Qt 6 versions; after a Qt upgrade, `TestHiDpiQt::initTestCase` fails loudly if the two screens do not come up with ratios 1 and 2.
